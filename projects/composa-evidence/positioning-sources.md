# Competitive opportunity map

Content source: Samuel's [Strategy & Product Mental Model](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-s2QnS2rXmY8hK-Aib3hMTP08y8dG8urYiRbSE1hPFw/edit?tab=t.wtv5w6m0dtgb), section 4, read 2026-09-03.

The diagram preserves four categories from the source table: animation presets,
AI asset generation, screen recording/explainers, and text-based editing.
AI Abstracted Controls is omitted at Samuel's explicit request.
This is a qualitative opportunity map, not a measured spectrum or a claim that
the proposed capabilities have shipped. Animation is the V1 foundation; the
remaining categories are future opportunities. No research document was edited.
Samuel's 2026-09-03 annotations add CleanShot to screen explainers and Descript
to asset creation, shorten the Descript label, and replace the root-and-branch
map with a light four-group spectrum treatment inspired by Rem. The diagram's
line groups workflows; it does not encode a quantitative ranking.
